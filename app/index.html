Watch out: Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy that can be missed. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house — before you leave Chapter 1. See its guide below.

    Play in this order. Estimated time to the platinum: about 200 hours (KeenGamer) to 300 hours (Push Square), difficulty 4/10. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there is no mission replay, and cheats disable trophies and saving while active.
